Where Demoiselle Pixel gets her ideas

Demoiselle Pixel curates a modern salon for the realms of art and technology, offering secretful insights into the makers and methods behind CG. Her approach is poised, intelligent, and richly artistic, inviting audiences into a world lit by curiosity and craftsmanship.

Bilingual Creative Specialist and Professor, Demoiselle Pixel provides refined training and demos on tools like Katana, Mari, and Nuke, and teaches LookDev, Lighting, Rendering and Compositing at University. With experience across global studios and acclaimed productions, she brings clarity, warmth, and feminine sophistication to the craft of making movies, series, games, and animation.
