Meet Sophie

Hey, it's Sophie. Let me save you a few years.

I spent eight years lighting and compositing shows at Atomic Cartoons, WildBrain, Mainframe and ICON Creative Studio. I learned most of it the hard way: on deadline, by breaking things, and by asking the questions everyone else was too nervous to ask.

Then I moved to Foundry and spent three years helping more than 40 studios on two continents use their tools. I got to see how the same problems show up everywhere, and how differently each studio solves them. That's the part you can't get from a tutorial, and it's what I put in every lesson.

Since 2023 I've taught 3D animation and pipeline at Emily Carr University. Every term I watch talented people hit the same wall I hit. You don't need to be special to get through it. You need someone to show you the map.

Everyone can do this. Including you.

Questions

Before you choose.

I'm a complete beginner. Is there a course for me?

Yes. The Inside is written for you: someone getting ready for art school, or teaching themselves on a budget. You don't need any studio experience. If you feel lost right now, you won't by the end.

Which course should I start with?

Start with the one that matches where you are today. Starting out: The Inside. Applying and hearing nothing: The Breakthrough. Comfortable with the basics and ready for procedural work: Course 03. Want to build the tools and bring AI into the pipeline: Course 04. Not sure? Pick "Not sure yet" on the waitlist and I'll point you to the right one.

Do I need to take them in order?

No. Each course stands on its own and never assumes you've seen another one.

What software do I need?

The Breakthrough needs none. The Inside uses Maya, Substance 3D Painter and Arnold, plus PureRef and Photoshop for concept art. Student and learning versions work for the exercises.

Do I get a certificate?

The Inside ends with a capstone project: the full elf, through every department. Finishing it earns your certificate of completion.

How much does it cost?

Each course is priced on its own. Prices go to the waitlist first, along with early-bird pricing.
